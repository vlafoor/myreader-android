The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo

Chapter Seven

She had always been reluctant to say what she truly wanted. There was something elusive about certainty, as though naming it might make it disappear.

The room was quiet enough that she could hear the faint turn of a page from the chair beside her. For a moment, she thought about all the things people keep because they are afraid to lose them.

Then she smiled, not because anything had become simple, but because the answer finally felt inevitable.
